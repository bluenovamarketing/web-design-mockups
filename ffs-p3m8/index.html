Questions

Good to know

Do you really come to our home?

Yes. All therapy is provided in your home, which lowers anxiety for your child and means new skills are practiced in the rooms and routines where they are actually needed.

Which insurance do you accept?

We accept Nevada Medicaid, Aetna, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Molina and Tricare, and we are becoming credentialed with all major insurances in the Clark County area. Self-pay rates are available, and we review your coverage before anything is scheduled.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Las Vegas, Henderson, North Las Vegas, Summerlin, Spring Valley, Enterprise, Paradise, Green Valley, Anthem and the surrounding Clark County communities.

What ages and needs do you support?

We work with children and teens. Occupational therapy covers fine motor, sensory processing, self-regulation, daily living skills and school readiness. Speech therapy covers articulation, language development, social communication and feeding.

How long does a session last?

Most sessions run 45 to 60 minutes and are scheduled around school, work and your family's routine, including evening visits.

Do I need a doctor's referral?

It depends on your plan. Some insurers require a referral or prescription from your pediatrician and some do not. We check this for you when we review your coverage.

What is the difference between occupational and speech therapy?

Occupational therapy builds the physical, sensory and self-care skills a child uses to take part in daily life. Speech therapy builds the ability to understand language and to be understood by others. Many children benefit from both.

How long will my child need therapy?

It depends on your child's goals and how often you are able to practice between visits. We set measurable goals at the evaluation and review progress with you regularly so you always know where things stand.
