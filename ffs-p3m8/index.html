Why in-home therapy?

Real progress happens where your child lives.

Comfort, familiarity and fewer trips mean more focus on what matters. Your therapist works with your child’s real routines, toys and spaces.

  • No driving across town or waiting rooms
  • Skills practiced in the places they’ll actually use them
  • Parents learn alongside their child
  • Support for school readiness, attention and following directions
  • Feeding help for picky eaters and sensory challenges
