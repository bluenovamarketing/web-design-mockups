Why in-home

Real progress happens where your child lives.

No driving across town, no waiting room. Your therapist works with your child’s real routines, toys and spaces, and teaches you how to keep going between visits.

  • Familiar surroundings reduce anxiety
  • Skills practiced exactly where they’re needed
  • Parents learn right alongside their child
  • Customized plans, easy strategies for every caregiver

Questions

Good to know

Do you really come to our home?

Yes. Therapy is provided in your home, which reduces anxiety and helps skills carry into daily life.

Which insurance do you accept?

Nevada Medicaid, Aetna, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Molina and Tricare. We’re also becoming credentialed with all major insurances in Clark County, and self-pay is available.

What areas do you serve?

Las Vegas, Clark County and surrounding areas.

What ages and needs do you support?

Kids and teens. We support everything from fine motor and sensory needs to language, regulation and school readiness. Call us to talk through your child’s situation.
