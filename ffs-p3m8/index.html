Why in-home therapy

Real progress happens where your child lives.

No waiting rooms, no driving across town. Your therapist works with your child’s real routines, toys and spaces – and teaches you how to keep going between visits.

  • Skills practiced in the places they’ll use them
  • Parents learn alongside their child
  • Support for focus, following directions and daily routines
  • Help for picky eating and sensory challenges
