Before you call

Common questions

How soon can we start?

Most families hear back the same business day. Because we come to you, there is no waiting for a treatment room to open up.

Do I need a doctor's referral?

It depends on your plan. We check when we review your insurance, so you do not have to work it out alone.

How long is a session?

Sessions usually run 45 to 60 minutes, scheduled around school, work and your family's routine.

Do I need to be home during therapy?

Yes, and we want you there. Parent coaching is part of every visit so progress continues all week.

What should we have ready for the first visit?

Nothing special. We work with the toys, furniture and routines you already have, because that is where the skills need to carry over.

What if my child refuses to participate?

That is common and it is information, not a setback. Sessions are play-based and we follow your child's lead until they are comfortable.
