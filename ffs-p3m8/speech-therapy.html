What we work on

Every plan is customized, these are common areas of focus.

Speech sounds

Articulation and phonological support so your child is easier to understand.

Language development

Building vocabulary, sentences, and understanding of what others say.

Social communication

Conversation, turn-taking and pragmatic language skills.

Feeding & oral-motor

Support for picky eating and oral-motor challenges.

Cognitive-communication

Recall, attending and comprehending instructions.

Parent coaching

Simple tips to boost communication all day long.

Is this right for my child?

You might consider a free call if your child…

  • Is hard for others to understand
  • Is late to talk or use sentences
  • Struggles to follow directions or answer questions
  • Has difficulty with back-and-forth conversation
