<section class=""><div class="wrap"><div class="center"><span class="tag">Why families call</span><h2>Therapy that fits the life you <span class="gold">actually live</span></h2></div><div class="prose rv" style="margin-top:44px">
<p class="lead">Most families reach us at the same point: something has been harder for their child than it should be, for longer than feels comfortable, and the waitlist at every clinic in town is months deep.</p>
<p>Family Flow Strategies was built to remove that wait. We are a pediatric occupational and speech therapy practice serving children and teens across Las Vegas and Clark County, and we come to you. No intake room, no commute across the valley after school, no sitting in a lobby while your child melts down before the session has even started.</p>
<p>Working in the home changes what therapy can accomplish. We see the actual doorway your child struggles to walk through, the real backpack that never gets packed, the kitchen table where homework falls apart every night. We work on those things in the place they happen, which is why the progress tends to stay.</p>
<p>It also changes your role. You are in the room, learning the same strategies we use, asking questions as they come up. By the time we leave, you have something practical to try before the next visit. Therapy becomes something your family does all week, not something that happens for forty-five minutes on a Thursday.</p>

<section class="mist"><div class="wrap"><div class="center"><span class="tag">Questions</span><h2>Good to know</h2></div><div class="faq rv"><details><summary>Do you really come to our home?</summary><p>Yes. All therapy is provided in your home, which lowers anxiety for your child and means new skills are practiced in the rooms and routines where they are actually needed.</p></details><details><summary>Which insurance do you accept?</summary><p>We accept Nevada Medicaid, Aetna, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Molina and Tricare, and we are becoming credentialed with all major insurances in the Clark County area. Self-pay rates are available, and we review your coverage before anything is scheduled.</p></details><details><summary>What areas do you serve?</summary><p>We serve Las Vegas, Henderson, North Las Vegas, Summerlin, Spring Valley, Enterprise, Paradise, Green Valley, Anthem and the surrounding Clark County communities.</p></details><details><summary>What ages and needs do you support?</summary><p>We work with children and teens. Occupational therapy covers fine motor, sensory processing, self-regulation, daily living skills and school readiness. Speech therapy covers articulation, language development, social communication and feeding.</p></details><details><summary>How long does a session last?</summary><p>Most sessions run 45 to 60 minutes and are scheduled around school, work and your family's routine, including evening visits.</p></details><details><summary>Do I need a doctor's referral?</summary><p>It depends on your plan. We check this for you when we review your coverage.</p></details><details><summary>What is the difference between occupational and speech therapy?</summary><p>Occupational therapy builds the physical, sensory and self-care skills a child uses to take part in daily life.
