<section class="mist"><div class="wrap"><div class="center"><span class="tag">Our philosophy</span><h2>Small skills, <span class="gold">real lives</span></h2></div><div class="prose rv" style="margin-top:44px">
<p class="lead">We measure progress by what changes at home, not by what improves on a worksheet.</p>
<p>A child who can finally manage buttons has not just mastered a fine motor task. They have bought back ten minutes of a stressful morning and a piece of their own independence. A teenager who learns to ask for clarification instead of shutting down has not just practiced a communication strategy. They have changed how a classroom feels to them.</p>
<p>That is the lens we bring to every plan. We set goals a parent would actually notice, explain the reasoning behind each activity in plain language, and adjust as your child grows. We tell you honestly when something is working and when it is time to try a different approach.</p>
<p>We also believe families deserve straight answers about cost and coverage before anything begins. We review your insurance up front, explain what is and is not covered, and give you a clear picture of what to expect.</p>
