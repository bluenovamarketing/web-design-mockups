About

Meet the Family Flow team

Caring, experienced therapists who treat every child like family.

Owner & Occupational Therapist

Andrew Jackson

Andrew set out to specialize in hand rehabilitation, but a pediatric placement changed everything. Skeptical at first, he thrived and found a passion for empowering children, especially those facing behavioral challenges.

He understands kids and teens not only professionally but from a deeply personal place, and it is his mission to pay forward the support he received. Andrew earned his Master’s in Occupational Therapy and served his community in Utah as an occupational therapist before bringing in-home care to Las Vegas families.

  • Master’s in Occupational Therapy
  • Pediatric focus: sensory, fine motor, regulation, daily routines
  • Previously served families in Utah
Our philosophy

Small skills, real lives

We measure progress by what changes at home, not by what improves on a worksheet.

A child who can finally manage buttons has not just mastered a fine motor task. They have bought back ten minutes of a stressful morning and a piece of their own independence. A teenager who learns to ask for clarification instead of shutting down has not just practiced a communication strategy. They have changed how a classroom feels to them.

That is the lens we bring to every plan. We set goals a parent would actually notice, explain the reasoning behind each activity in plain language, and adjust as your child grows. We tell you honestly when something is working and when it is time to try a different approach.

We also believe families deserve straight answers about cost and coverage before anything begins. We review your insurance up front, explain what is and is not covered, and give you a clear picture of what to expect.
